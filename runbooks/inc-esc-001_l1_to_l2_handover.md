# INC-ESC-001: L1 to L2 handover
Used when there is no matching runbook, a step failed, verification failed, or the culprit is outside the allowed actions.
Steps (done by the agents, not shown as a table): collect evidence; assign the ticket to the group and set In Progress; add work notes (symptom, evidence, what was tried, what is uncertain); notify the team channel.
